# Store screenshot checklist

Capture on a physical device or high-res simulator (phone + optional tablet).

## Required frames (both stores)

1. Login / OTP entry  
2. Welcome hub with coaches  
3. Live coach chat (Kai or Maya)  
4. Vision / photo analyze flow (gallery or camera picker)  
5. Streak / check-in  
6. Leaderboard  
7. Trophy road / market  
8. Settings (privacy + terms links visible)  
9. Pricing **native banner** (“Manage on kaifyai.org”) — proves no in-app IAP claim  

## Listing URLs

- Privacy: `https://kaifyai.org/privacy`
- Terms: `https://kaifyai.org/terms`

Store assets are **not** committed to git (binary size). Keep them in the release drive / App Store Connect / Play Console.
